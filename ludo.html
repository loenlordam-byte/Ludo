Ludo

Pick your seats, then start the game.
• Roll a 6 to bring a piece out. A 6 gives you another roll (three 6s in a row loses the turn).
• Landing on a rival piece sends it home and gives you a bonus roll — except on ★ and coloured start squares, which are safe.
• Reaching the centre also gives a bonus roll. You need the exact number to finish. First to get all 4 pieces home wins.
